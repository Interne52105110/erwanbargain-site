Before you move

What pay does not show: when health coverage starts, how much leave the law guarantees, what childcare costs, what the move itself costs, what happens to pension rights, and how long the commute is.

Rules and amounts read on official pages in November 2026. They change: check them at the source before deciding. What could not be confirmed is listed at the bottom of the page, not presented as fact.

When public health coverage starts

Québec
Up to 3 months after registration. No wait for people arriving from France, Belgium or Luxembourg (social security agreements), with a certificate from their home scheme. A work permit must be valid for more than 6 months.
Ontario
No waiting period since March 2020. A work permit holder needs a full-time job of at least 6 months.
British Columbia
Rest of the month of arrival plus 2 months.
Alberta
From arrival, if the application is made within 3 months.
Manitoba, New Brunswick, Nova Scotia, Newfoundland and Labrador
No wait for people arriving from abroad who qualify (a work permit of 12 months is generally required).
Yukon
End of the second month after the month of arrival.
France
An employee is covered from the first day of work.
Switzerland
Three months to take out the mandatory insurance, which then applies from the day of arrival. Ordinary deductible 300 CHF; co-payment capped at 700 CHF a year for an adult.

In Canada, public insurance does not cover prescription drugs outside hospital, dental or eye care for working-age adults. Québec requires drug insurance: the public plan costs up to 789 CAD a year in premium for anyone without a private plan.

What childcare costs parents

Québec
9.65 CAD a day in subsidised childcare in 2026. 30,688 children were waiting for a place in May 2025, the last figure published.
Ontario
Capped at 22 CAD a day, 19 CAD on average, in regulated childcare.
Manitoba, Saskatchewan, Prince Edward Island, Newfoundland and Labrador, Nunavut
10 CAD a day.
Alberta
15 CAD a day; the federal agreement runs only to March 2027.
Luxembourg
20 free hours a week for children aged 1 to 4, 46 weeks a year; free care and meals for school-age children during school weeks.
Geneva (city)
Income-based: from 15 CHF to 265 CHF a month for each weekly day of attendance.

What the move costs

Canada, permanent residence
1,590 CAD per adult (990 processing + 600 right of permanent residence) and 270 CAD per child: 3,720 CAD for a couple with two children.
Canada, work permit
155 CAD, plus 100 CAD for an open permit; biometrics 85 CAD a person or 170 CAD a family.
Canada, proof of funds
Express Entry: 15,263 CAD for one person, 28,362 CAD for four. Francophone Community Immigration Pilot: 10,507 and 19,524 CAD. People already working in Canada are exempt.
Québec, selection
940 CAD for the main applicant and 201 CAD per family member; 233 CAD for a temporary worker.
France, residence permit
350 € for a first permit and 250 € for a renewal (non-EU nationals).
Rental deposit
France: one month unfurnished, two furnished. Luxembourg: two months at most. Ontario: one month, usable only as the last month’s rent.
Unemployment benefit
Can be carried for 3 months (up to 6) when moving within the EU and Switzerland. Nothing equivalent exists with Canada or Québec.

What happens to pension rights

  • Periods worked in several countries are added up to open the right to a pension, but each country pays only for the years worked there, by its own formula and at its own pension age. An agreement does not raise the amount.
  • France, Belgium, Luxembourg and Switzerland each have a social security agreement with Canada (covering Old Age Security and the Canada Pension Plan) and a separate understanding with Québec (covering the Québec Pension Plan).
  • Canada’s Old Age Security requires 10 years of residence after age 18 and is paid in fortieths. Someone arriving at 50 and staying to 65 gets 15/40 of it: about 286 CAD a month at late-2026 rates (our computation).
  • Swiss occupational pension (second pillar): the mandatory part cannot be taken in cash when leaving for an EU country, but the whole balance can when leaving for Canada.
