Cities and local areas

A region gives an idea; a city is more precise. This is the finest level each country publishes, with rent where it is known. At this level, no country publishes a breakdown by age and education.

Each country measures local unemployment in its own way. Figures compare within a country, not across countries.

“By education”: census unemployment rate for people with no diploma, a secondary diploma and higher education. It is a different measure from the local rate in the next column: self-declared, higher, and taken in May 2021 in Canada, during the pandemic. Use it to read the gap between education levels within a city, not to compare cities across countries.

Rent against pay: in Europe, against the national average net wage; in Canada, against gross median full-time earnings. The two bases do not compare, and these shares will be recomputed with local pay.
