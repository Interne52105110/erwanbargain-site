Territories covered

The French-speaking labour markets of Europe and Canada for which public statistics exist, at the finest level that stays reliable. French overseas departments are not covered.

Why some Canadian towns outside Québec are included

Outside Québec, the study includes the cities and regions that Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada (IRCC) selected, in two federal programmes, to attract and welcome French-speaking newcomers.

  • Francophone Community Immigration Pilot: a pathway to permanent residence, tied to a job offer from a designated employer in a participating community.
  • Welcoming Francophone Communities: funding for the reception and settlement of French-speaking newcomers. It is not an immigration pathway in itself.

These places are marked with a programme tag. Being listed means the community is open to francophone newcomers; it does not mean jobs are easy to get there, which is what the study measures.
